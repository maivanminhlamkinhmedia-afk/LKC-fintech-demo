The foundation is not a CMS-012 writer or DB execution proof.
- Pure CMS-011.0 policy f909aa3f5eca7ed56ed7337853ad6252f1ccb419 covers SUBMIT/TAKE/APPROVE, not CMS-012. Pure Q0 queue 83f799ee1f3db5a2fd8fec00b2d24e95adc1fca9 covers SUBMITTED only. These separate PR sources are dependencies, not assumed merged into main.

Existing roles have cms:article:review/approve, but no dedicated fact-check permission. Enum presence is not an approved transition, assignment policy or grant. The current creator draft writer permits edits in CHANGES_REQUESTED under updatedAt CAS; no request-changes action exists.
- CMS-007 source URL metadata does not prove a claim was fact-checked. Audience/Product and C02 paid-reader contracts do not authorize notes or paid body in a queue, error or public response. G0 A permits CMS-private specification; schema/migration, media cleanup, shared contracts and runtime release retain B/C gates.

## Proposed semantics, held until decisions

A fact-check finding is an assessment bound to a persisted ArticleVersion and server-authorized actor. It may be recorded without changing Article status only after actor, visibility and note lifecycle are approved. REQUEST_CHANGES is a distinct review decision on the active review version. Its candidate effect is to return the working Article to CHANGES_REQUESTED for authorized author edits while retaining immutable submitted version and history. This transition and prerequisites remain OPEN. APPROVE stays in CMS-011; REJECT is not an alias for REQUEST_CHANGES. Whether FACT_CHECK is a required state, optional state or assessment-only is OPEN. No runtime code may select a default.

| Operation | Proposed gate, not yet approved policy | Required invariant |
|---|---|---|
| Fact-check assessment | ACTIVE actor with existing review and scoped-read rights; source status and assignment OPEN | Typed articleId/versionId and actor/time; no version mutation or implicit approval/publication |
| REQUEST_CHANGES | Exact active same-Article version; actor/assignment/status and reason requirements OPEN | One atomic Article CAS plus version-bound decision/event; history preserved, stale approval cannot authorize edited content |
| Author edits | Existing own-Article update scope, CHANGES_REQUESTED status | Persist working Article via shared updatedAt CAS; never edit prior ArticleVersion or review rows |
| Resubmit | CMS-011 submit gates on fully persisted content/audience | New immutable version/review cycle; old approval is not reused |
| Approve/publish | Separate CMS-011/CMS-015 gates | Approval binds exact version/audience; publication later uses approved snapshot, not raw draft |

The existing role matrix gives SUPER_ADMIN/ADMIN review and read:any, CREATOR own submit/update, and no CMS review rights to ANALYST/MANAGER/CLIENT. Do not add a role or trust browser editorId. Whether TAKE/assignment is required, self-review is allowed, and what note text an author may see are OPEN. Undecided operations and fields fail closed.

## Identity, concurrency, transaction and recovery

Every new assessment, note, decision and event needs typed articleId plus versionId and a same-Article FK/verification. New writes cannot use legacy null version links. The server reloads an ACTIVE actor and scoped Article inside one Serializable transaction, verifies current Article.updatedAt to the millisecond, status, activeReviewVersionId, expected version, and any approved assignment rule. A scoped compare-and-swap decides competing reviewer writes; at most one wins. basisUpdatedAt identifies the persisted revision captured by the version; it does not replace the working Article CAS. Client role, title, audience, editorId or clock are never authoritative.

The approved request-changes transition must atomically update Article status/token/pointers and append its version-bound decision/event; failure anywhere rolls back all of it. PO/Integrator still must decide whether activeReviewVersionId is retained or cleared and precisely when activeApprovalVersionId is invalidated. Neither choice may permit old approval to authorize new content. Any writer changing body, source, classification, cover or audience after approval must invalidate active approval in that same writer transaction; previously published version remains on its own CMS-015 pointer. Historical ArticleVersion/review/finding rows stay append-only by application policy; a DB FK does not enforce immutability.

Safe failures: NOT_FOUND for out-of-scope identity; FORBIDDEN for role/session loss; VALIDATION_ERROR for malformed input; NOT_EDITABLE for wrong state; EDIT_CONFLICT for Article CAS; STALE_REVIEW_VERSION for pointer/version mismatch; POLICY_UNRESOLVED for undecided business policy; INTERNAL_ERROR without DB details. Lost ACK means UNKNOWN_OUTCOME: stop writes, read authorized exact version/operation event and scoped Article state, preserve input, and never blindly resend. A post-commit cache/revalidation error is success with warning, not a reason to repeat mutation. Operation correlation must be typed and scoped, not hidden in comment or arbitrary JSON.

## Privacy

Server projections are allowlists per actor/context. Internal fact-check notes, restricted reviewer identity, media path/receipt and paid body never appear in public HTML, RSC/Flight, queue DTO, shared cache, logs or errors. A creator-facing response excludes internal notes until PO approves an explicit author-visible projection; it still requires own-Article and exact-version scope. Reviewer reads require a fresh read:any + review scope and version check, not mere possession of an ID. Cache private reads safely using the installed Next guide at implementation time. Verify response bytes and network attempts, not just hidden UI. A source URL or note does not cause automatic external fetch.
